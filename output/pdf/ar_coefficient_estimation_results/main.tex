\section*{Final coefficient estimation}
For each known order $p\in\{1,2,3,4,5\}$, 100 Jones coefficient vectors were
sampled and reused across five Beta innovation shapes, giving 2500 series of
length $T=1000$. Innovations have population mean zero and variance $1/3$;
2000 burn-in observations were discarded~\cite{experiment,implementation,jones}.

The per-process coefficient error is
\[
e_l(p)=\frac{1}{p}\sum_{j=1}^{p}
  \left|\widehat{\phi}^{(l)}_j-\phi^{(l)}_j\right|,
\qquad l=1,\ldots,100.
\]
Tables report the mean of $e_l(p)$ over the 100 processes in each
condition~\cite{implementation}. All finite errors, including large errors and
errors from nonstationary estimates, are included. Column minima are shown in
bold. Table formatting follows Table~2.1 of the compiled thesis~\cite{style}.

\subsection*{Experiment details}
The true order is supplied to every estimator. OLS and Horv\'ath's LP use no
intercept; Yule--Walker uses centered observations. Minimum width uses the fixed
start $e_1$ and diameter-normalized steps
\[
\gamma_m=\frac{0.1}{L}\,2^{-m/100},
\]
where $L$ is the empirical point-cloud diameter. The cap is 2000 evaluated
states, patience is 200, and the cumulative patience-reset threshold is
$\delta=10^{-8}$. Every strict width improvement is retained; no restarts or
stationarity filtering are used~\cite{implementation}.

All 10000 fits completed without fitting errors~\cite{experiment}. The companion
\texttt{results.csv} contains the unrounded results; \texttt{provenance.json}
records the source files and checksums.
