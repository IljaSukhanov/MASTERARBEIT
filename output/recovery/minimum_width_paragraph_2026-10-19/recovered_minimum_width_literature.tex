The problem of determining the minimum width of a point cloud is well known in the computational geometry literature. More specifically, one looks for two parallel hyperplanes enclosing all points with smallest distance to each other. \cite{10.1145/323233.323234} gives algorithms for exact computation together with runtime complexity, but only for two and three dimensional point clouds.
