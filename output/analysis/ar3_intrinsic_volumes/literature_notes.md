# Preliminary novelty assessment: Schur–Cohn intrinsic volumes

Assessment date: 16 October 2026.

## Scope

The candidate object is the closure of the **full real Schur–Cohn coefficient
region**, with the Euclidean coefficient metric and the normal-cycle /
Lipschitz–Killing extension of intrinsic volumes. The convex hull and the subset
of polynomials having only real roots are different objects.

## Verified nearby literature

1. Kirschenhofer and Thuswaldner, *Distribution results on polynomials with bounded
   roots*, §1, equation (1.1): the full region's ordinary volume is known in every
   dimension, attributed there to Fam. This is not a candidate novelty.
   <https://arxiv.org/pdf/1609.06947>

2. Dzhafarov, Büyükköroğlu, and Akyar, *Stability Region for Discrete Time Systems
   and Its Boundary* (2021), pp. 246–255: the publisher abstract describes a
   parametrization of the entire boundary by three multilinear images of boxes.
   Boundary parametrization itself is existing work.
   <https://journal.imm.uran.ru/2021-v.27-3-pp.246-255>
   DOI: 10.21538/0134-4889-2021-27-3-246-255.

3. Kukoverov, *Towards Computation of Surface Area of Schur Stability Domain*
   (2025), pp. 113–133: the abstract specifies the subset whose roots are all real.
   The indexed introduction on p. 113 describes determining the full domain's
   hypersurface area as an open question. This concerns the general problem; it
   does not establish that the three-dimensional special case is unpublished.
   <https://ait.mtas.ru/en/archive/volume86issue2/ARC%2002-2025%20002Kukoverov.pdf>
   DOI: 10.31857/S0005117925020025.

4. Dutertre, *Euler characteristic and Lipschitz-Killing curvatures of closed
   semi-algebraic sets*, §2: the general curvature framework is already available.
   Applying it to this family might yield new explicit results, but defining
   curvatures using normal cycles is not a new construction.
   <https://arxiv.org/pdf/1002.0767>

## Search result and its limits

Targeted public-web searches combined Schur–Cohn / Schur stability region /
autoregressive coefficient region with intrinsic volumes, surface area, mean
curvature, total mean curvature, mean breadth, Minkowski, quermassintegrals,
Lipschitz–Killing, and curvature measures. No directly matching paper giving the
full intermediate intrinsic-volume sequence was located. Searches for our
three-dimensional surface-area digits did not locate matching prior work.

This is evidence of a possible literature gap, not proof of priority. In
particular, the public indexed abstract and introduction of the 2025 paper were
available, but the full PDF could not be retrieved during this check. An
exhaustive bibliographic-database search and complete backward/forward citation
review have not been performed.

## Research judgment

The intermediate curvatures of the full regions are a plausible research
direction. The three-dimensional V1 and V2 formulas in `derivation.md` are
candidate explicit results; the numerical checks in `results.json` are supporting
evidence. No novelty claim should yet be attached to them.

A research presentation would need a rigorous justification that the boundary
formula computes the chosen normal-cycle curvatures, including the singular
vertices, followed by a closer priority check. General-dimensional recurrences,
exact formulas, bounds, or asymptotics would provide a stronger prospective
contribution than decimal values alone. These are recommendations, not claimed
results.
