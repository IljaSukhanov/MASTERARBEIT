The evaluation of quadratic kernel functionals through the empirical Fourier
power spectrum has a precedent in~\cite[Section~3.5, especially
Equations~(3.51), (3.56) and~(3.61)]{silverman_density_1986}.
For the construction considered here, the values $|P_n(\omega_k)|^2$ can be
computed once and reused for every bandwidth pair. With $K+1$ frequency
nodes, direct evaluation of these values requires $\mathcal{O}(nK)$
operations, while assembling all Gram entries requires
$\mathcal{O}(N^2K)$ operations. Thus, the total cost is
$\mathcal{O}(nK+N^2K)$, compared with $\mathcal{O}(N^2n^2)$ for direct
evaluation of the exact pair sums. The accuracy depends on both the
frequency cutoff and the quadrature resolution. Related comparisons of
direct and FFT-based computation in bandwidth selection are given
in~\cite[Sections~4--6]{gramacki_fft_2017}; their method uses data binning
and discrete convolution, whereas the approximation above directly
discretizes the frequency integral.
